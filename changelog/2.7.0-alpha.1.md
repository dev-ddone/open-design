# 2.7.0-alpha.1 — Client review workflow

## Added

- Design comments associated with a project, page or Fabric object.
- Comment resolution and reopening.
- Review lifecycle: draft, in review, changes requested and approved.
- Reviewer notes and author/reviewer metadata.
- Editor panel for comments and decisions.
- Migration `005_design_reviews.sql`.
- Runtime smoke test for comment persistence, resolution and approval.

## Permissions

- Accessible users may read and add comments to designs they can access.
- Comment authors and editors may resolve discussions.
- Effective editor access is required to change review status.

## Known limitations

- Mentions and email notifications are not yet delivered.
- Review status does not yet block export automatically.
- Comments are object-linked but do not render as pins over the canvas.
