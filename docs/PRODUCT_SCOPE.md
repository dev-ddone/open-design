# Product scope

DDone Design is a self-hosted collaborative platform for **static visual design**.

## Included product families

- social graphics and campaign variants;
- menus, price lists, flyers, posters and print layouts;
- presentations;
- brand-controlled client templates;
- editable tables, grids, frames, vectors, photographs and text;
- collaborative review, versioning and private asset management;
- PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF workflows.

## Explicitly outside scope

The product does not target video editing, animated timelines, audio editing, GIF animation tooling or 3D scene editing. These categories must not be added to navigation, marketing copy, API capability lists or acceptance criteria unless the scope is deliberately changed in a future architecture decision record.

## Competitive position

The goal is not to copy every Canva surface. The goal is to be stronger for agencies and organizations that need self-hosting, client separation, controlled templates, private assets, traceable licensing, brand governance and open integrations.
